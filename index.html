The story

Sami’s Gen-Z younger sibling

Little Flower was opened by Ali Zaman, whose family has run the beloved Sami’s Kabab House down the block for years. Where Sami’s is a neighborhood institution for Afghan kababs, Little Flower is its quieter, brighter counterpart — a coffee-and-pastry counter that carries the same roots into a new register.

Everything on the menu is halal, and everything on the menu still tastes like somewhere specific: rose water, cardamom, turmeric, and pistachio show up in the coffee, the doughnuts, and the sandwiches alike, sitting comfortably next to the short rib and the soft scrambled egg sammie.

The room

Small, bright, and worth the trip

The space is compact — blonde wood, gray slab tables, and warm industrial lighting, just under the elevated tracks. Seating is limited and it fills up fast on weekend mornings, so this isn't a park-yourself-with-a-laptop-all-day kind of place. It's a get-a-great-coffee-and-a-pastry, maybe stay for a sandwich, kind of place.

  • Halal menu throughout
  • Outdoor seating, weather permitting
  • Dog-friendly patio
  • Takeout & free Wi-Fi
